# RUST BUCKET RISING — Quick Rules Reference (GenCon playtest build)

*Win: first to **25 Fame** triggers the final round — every other player gets one last turn, then
highest Fame wins. (Ties: most hazards held, then most missions, then most credits.)*

## Your turn
1. **Start** — your installed cards fire; your captain's ability triggers; reveal any hazards in hand.
2. **Actions** — any number, any order: play cards · move · complete the mission where you are ·
   buy/install at a station · use system abilities · clear hazards.
3. **Cleanup** — discard your hand and played cards, draw 5.

## The two economies — learn this first
- ⚡ **POWER CARRIES OVER between turns** (max 6 per system). Playing power cards *banks* power;
  it's only spent when you use it. Your dials stay where they are at end of turn.
- **⚡ with no system named goes wherever you choose.** If a card names a system, it goes there.
- **CREDITS RESET every turn.** Unspent credits are lost — spend them the turn you earn them.

## Systems (spend power once per ability per turn)
| System | Ability | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| **Weapons** | **2⚡**: give a hazard to a player at your location | **4⚡**: give a hazard to anyone |
| **Computers** | 1⚡: draw 1 card | 3⚡: draw 3, keep 1 |
| **Engines** | 1⚡: move 1 space — no once-per-turn limit | — |
| **Logistics** | 1⚡: gain 1 credit | 3⚡: trash a card |

## Moving & the board
Moving 1 space costs 1 Engine power (or a free move). Stations at **1 / 3 / 5** sell Tier 1 / 2 / 3
cards. Missions sit at every location — those at 2–6 are **face-down until someone visits**.

## Markets
Once per turn at a station, you may **look through one stack** — it stays open afterward. You may
buy **any card** from an open stack (Tier 1 stacks are always open). Installing costs the card's install cost; **one install per system**
(new installs may replace old ones). Installed cards trigger every turn.

> ★ **Premium cards score:** any card costing **5+ credits awards Fame when you buy it** —
> **+1** at cost 5–6, **+2** at cost 7.

## Missions
Be there, pay the printed power, gain the Fame and reward. **Gear rewards start working the turn
you earn them** (take the benefit immediately, then every turn start). Completed missions are
replaced at end of turn — one completion per location per turn.

## Hazards
Given hazards go to your **discard** — they're active while in your **hand**. Clear one by paying
its printed cost on your turn. **Game end: −1 Fame for every hazard still anywhere in your cards.**
