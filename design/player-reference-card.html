Your Turn

1

START — installed cards & captain trigger · reveal hazards in your hand

2

ACTIONS — any number, any order: play cards · move · use system abilities · complete the mission here · buy / install (at a station) · clear hazards

3

CLEANUP — discard your hand and played cards · draw 5

The Two Economies

⚡ POWER CARRIES OVER between turns (max 6 per system). It is only lost when you spend it.

◉ CREDITS VANISH at end of turn — spend them now.

⚡ with no system named goes wherever you choose.

System Abilities (each once per turn)

WEAPONS2⚡ give a hazard to a player at your location
4⚡ give a hazard to anyone
COMPUTERS1⚡ draw 1 card  ·  3⚡ draw 3, keep 1
ENGINES1⚡ move 1 space — no limit, pay per space
LOGISTICS1⚡ gain 1 credit  ·  3⚡ trash a card

Moving

Each space costs 1 Engines ⚡ — or a free move from a card or captain.

Markets  (stations 1 / 3 / 5 = Tier 1 / 2 / 3)

  • Once per turn at a station: look through one stack — it stays open
  • Buy any card from an open stack (Tier 1 is always open) — bought cards go to your discard
  • ★ on a card = gain that Fame the moment you buy it

Installing

  • Pay the install cost · one install per system (a new one replaces the old)
  • Installed cards trigger every turn start

Missions

  • Be there · spend the printed power · gain the Fame + reward
  • Missions are face-down until someone visits that location
  • Gear starts working the turn you earn it — one gear slot per system, and only another gear replaces it

Hazards

  • Active while in your hand · clear one on your turn by paying its printed cost
  • Game end: −1 Fame for every hazard anywhere in your cards

Winning

  • 25 Fame → every other player takes one last turn → highest Fame wins
  • Ties: most hazards held → most missions → most credits
