## 1. Welcome, Contestant!

Congratulations. By reading this, you have already signed the waiver. There is no going back.

**Rust Bucket Rising** is the galaxy's highest-rated competitive survival game show. Each season, 2-4 contestants are dropped into the black with nothing but a salvaged junker ship, a starter deck of barely-functional equipment, and whatever wits they brought from home. Your mission: complete increasingly dangerous objectives, upgrade your heap of scrap into something resembling a spacecraft, sabotage your rivals, and earn enough **Fame** to be declared the season's winner.

The show's producers would like to remind you that your safety is *technically* a priority.

The contestant with the most **Fame** at the end of the final round wins the season and gets to keep their ship. Everyone else gets a commemorative mug and a shuttle ride home. Assuming they're still alive.

## 2. The Show Format

- **2-4 contestants** compete simultaneously
- **6 locations** along a linear space track, divided into three broadcast zones
- The game is played in **rounds** (one turn per contestant per round)
- The first contestant to reach **25 Fame** triggers the **final round** — everyone else gets one last turn
- After the final round, the contestant with the **most Fame wins**

### The Space Track

| Location | Zone | Features |
|----------|------|----------|
| 1 | Near Space | Station 1 (Tier 1 Market) + Mission |
| 2 | Near Space | Mission only |
| 3 | Mid Space | Station 3 (Tier 2 Market) + Mission |
| 4 | Mid Space | Mission only |
| 5 | Deep Space | Station 5 (Tier 3 Market) + Mission |
| 6 | Deep Space | Mission only |

All contestants start at **Location 1**. Missions at your starting location begin face-up; all others are face-down until you arrive.

*Section 2.1: The producers reserve the right to adjust locations mid-season for "narrative purposes." This has never happened. Officially.*

## 3. Your Ship

Every contestant's ship has four systems. Each system starts at **1 power** and can be upgraded to a maximum of **6 power**. Power resets to your base values at the start of each turn.

| System | Color | What It Does |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Weapons** (red) | Attack | Spend 1: Give a hazard to a contestant at your location. Spend 3: Give a hazard to any contestant. |
| **Computers** (blue) | Intelligence | Spend 1: Draw 1 card. Spend 3: Draw 3, keep 1, discard the rest. |
| **Engines** (orange) | Movement | Spend 1: Move 1 space along the track. |
| **Logistics** (green) | Economy | Spend 1: Gain 1 credit. Spend 3: Trash 1 card from your hand or discard pile. |

Power is a currency. You spend it to activate your systems, and you need it to complete missions. Cards and installations can boost your power beyond starting values each turn.

*Section 3.1: "Power" is measured in standardized galactic energy units. The show's measurement equipment has a margin of error of approximately 40%. This is considered acceptable.*

---

## 4. Your Deck

You start with a deck of **10 cards**:

- **6x Supply Check** — +1 Credit
- **3x Cheap Battery** — +1 power to any system
- **1x Basic Engines** — +2 Engines power

At the start of the game (and after each turn), you draw a hand of **5 cards**. When your deck runs out, shuffle your discard pile to form a new deck.

Your deck will grow as you buy new cards from station markets. It may also grow in... less pleasant ways (see: **Hazards**).

---

## 5. Turn Structure

Each turn has three phases. You cannot skip phases.

### Phase 1: Initial Phase

At the start of your turn:
1. Your power resets to base values (starting power + captain bonuses)
2. Your installed cards activate, granting their effects
3. Your captain ability triggers (if applicable)
4. If you have any installations that require a choice (power allocation), you make those choices now

### Phase 2: Action Phase

This is the main phase. You may take **any number of actions** in **any order**:

- **Play a card** from your hand (apply its effects immediately)
- **Move** your ship (spend Engine power or use free moves)
- **Activate a system** (spend power for the system's ability)
- **Buy a card** from a station market (if at a station with enough credits)
- **Buy & Install** a card in one action (pay buy cost + install cost)
- **Install a card** from your hand (pay install cost in credits)
- **Complete a mission** at your current location (if you meet the requirements)
- **Clear a hazard** from your hand (pay the hazard's clear cost)
- **Use your captain ability** (once per turn, if applicable)

There is no limit to how many cards you can play or actions you can take. Go wild. The cameras are rolling.

### Phase 3: Cleanup Phase

1. All remaining hand cards and played cards go to your **discard pile**
2. Your power, credits, moves, and temporary bonuses reset
3. Draw a fresh hand of **5 cards**
4. Your turn ends; play passes to the next contestant

## 6. Playing Cards

To play a card, select it from your hand. It moves to your "played" area, and its effects resolve immediately.

Card effects include:
- **Credits** (+1, +2, etc.) — currency for buying and installing
- **Power** (+2 Engines, +1 to any system, etc.) — boosts your ship systems
- **Draw** (+1 Card, +2 Cards) — draw additional cards from your deck
- **Moves** (+1 Move) — move spaces without spending Engine power
- **Hazards** (Give 1 hazard) — attack another contestant
- **Discounts** (Buy -1, Install -1, Mission -1) — reduce costs this turn
- **Trash** (May trash 1) — permanently remove a card from the game
- **Interactions** — force opponents to discard, lose power, or return installations (see Section 10)

Some cards grant **Fame** when purchased (not when played). This is noted on the card.

---

## 7. Stations & Markets

Stations are located at positions **1**, **3**, and **5** on the track. Each station has a market selling cards:

| Station | Location | Tier | Card Power Level |
|---------|----------|------|-----------------|
| Station 1 | Near Space | Tier 1 | Core upgrades (Cost: 3) |
| Station 3 | Mid Space | Tier 2 | Advanced systems (Cost: 4-6) |
| Station 5 | Deep Space | Tier 3 | Elite tech (Cost: 6-8) |

### Buying Cards

You must be **at the station's location** to buy from it. Pay the card's credit cost (minus any buy discounts). The card goes to your **discard pile** (you'll draw it in a future turn).

Market cards are displayed in **face-down stacks**. You can spend 1 Engine power or reveal them for free at your location. Only the top card of each revealed stack is available for purchase.

### Buy & Install

If a card has an install cost, you can buy it and install it in one action by paying both costs combined. The card goes directly into an installation slot instead of your discard pile.

*Section 7.1: Station markets are restocked between seasons. Mid-season shortages are "by design" and "not the producers' fault."*

## 8. Installations

Many cards can be **installed** onto one of your four ship systems. Installing a card gives you a **persistent bonus** that activates every turn during the Initial Phase.

To install a card from your hand:
1. The card must have an install cost
2. Pay the install cost in credits (minus any install discounts)
3. Choose which system slot to install it in
4. The card is placed in that system's installation slot

Each system has **one installation slot**. Installing a new card in an occupied slot sends the previous installation to your discard pile.

Installed cards are **not in your deck** — they sit on your ship board and provide their install effect every turn until replaced.

*Section 8.1: Installations are considered "permanent modifications" under show rules. The show accepts no liability for modifications that result in catastrophic system failure, spontaneous combustion, or sentience.*

## 9. Missions

Every location on the track has a **mission card**. Missions require specific power levels across your systems and reward **Fame** (plus bonus effects).

### Completing a Mission

To complete a mission at your current location:
1. The mission must be **revealed** (face-up)
2. You must meet all **power requirements** (your current power in each system must meet or exceed the listed values)
3. The required power is **spent** (subtracted from your current power)
4. You gain the mission's **Fame** and any **rewards**

After completing a mission, a new mission is drawn from the appropriate zone pool.

### Mission Zones & Difficulty

| Zone | Locations | Fame Range | Difficulty |
|------|-----------|------------|-----------|
| Near Space | 1-2 | 2 Fame | Low power requirements |
| Mid Space | 3-4 | 3-4 Fame | Moderate requirements |
| Deep Space | 5-6 | 5-6 Fame | High requirements |

### Mission Rewards

Missions grant one of three reward types:

- **Bolt** — Immediate power or credit bonuses
- **Gear** — An ongoing conditional bonus (e.g., "+1 power if at a station") that activates each turn
- **Trophy** — A passive ability that triggers on specific events (e.g., "gain 1 credit when you trash a card")

Some missions also have **end-game penalties** — a cost for keeping the reward. Read the fine print.

### Mission Discounts

Some cards grant a **mission discount** that reduces all power requirements by the discount amount (minimum 0 per system). This makes harder missions accessible earlier.

*Section 9.1: Mission objectives are real. The danger is real. The "safety net" mentioned in your contract refers to an actual net. It is decorative.*

## 10. Player Interactions

Several cards allow direct interference with other contestants. The show *encourages* this behavior.

### Interaction Cards

| Card | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Signal Jam** | Draw 1. Opponent at your location discards 1 (they choose). |
| **Data Breach** | See opponent's hand at your location. You choose 1 card they discard. +1 power. |
| **Ramming Speed** | +1 Move. Opponent at your location loses 1 power (they choose system). |
| **Tractor Beam** | Pull an opponent from an adjacent location to yours. +1 power. |
| **Repo Order** | +1 Credit. Opponent at your location returns an installed card (they choose). |
| **Contract Breach** | +1 power. Opponent at your location: they discard 2 OR you uninstall one of their cards (you choose mode). |

Interaction effects only trigger if a valid target exists. If multiple valid targets exist, you choose who to target.

*Section 10.1: Sabotage between contestants is permitted, encouraged, and frequently sponsored. Retaliation is expected. Personal vendettas make great television.*

## 11. Hazards

Hazards are bad cards that get shuffled into your deck. They clog your hand, waste your draws, and impose penalties when drawn. They are the show's way of keeping things "interesting."

### How You Get Hazards

- Another contestant **gives** you one using their Weapons system or a card effect
- Hazards go into your **discard pile** and eventually get shuffled into your deck

### Hazard Effects

When a hazard is in your hand, it takes up a card slot and imposes a restriction. Some hazards trigger immediately when drawn.

| Hazard | Effect | How to Clear |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Scrambled Controls | System abilities cost +1 power | Discard 2 cards |
| Thruster Jam | Can't move more than 1 space | Spend all Engines power (min 2) |
| Corrupted Nav Chip | Can't complete missions | 2 Computers power |
| Warrant Issued | Missions cost +2 Credits | 2 Credits + 1 Logistics power |
| Static Overload | Lose 1 power from a system (on draw) | 2 Computers power |
| Rogue AI Fragment | Can't install cards | 1 Computers power + 1 Credit |
| Smuggled Parasite | Triggers on draw, passes when cleared | Discard 1 + 1 Logistics power |
| Failsafe Lockdown | Max 2 cards this turn | 1 power from 2 different systems |
| Overloaded Circuits | Max +2 power from cards this turn | 1 power from 2 different systems |
| Corrosive Spores | Can't install cards | 2 Logistics power |

### Clearing Hazards

During your Action Phase, you can clear a hazard from your hand by paying its **clear cost**. The hazard is removed from the game (not just discarded).

### End-Game Hazard Penalty

At the end of the game, every hazard remaining in your deck costs you **-1 Fame**. Keep your ship clean, or pay the price.

*Section 11.1: Hazards are sourced from "naturally occurring space phenomena" and are "definitely not planted by the production team for dramatic effect." The producers' legal team was very clear about this.*

## 13. Winning the Show

### Triggering the Finale

When any contestant reaches **25 Fame** at the end of their turn, the **final round** begins. Every other contestant gets exactly **one more turn**. Then the game ends.

### Final Scoring

At the end of the game:
1. Each hazard remaining in your deck: **-1 Fame**
2. Any end-game mission penalties are applied
3. The contestant with the **most Fame wins**

In case of a tie, the tied contestant with fewer hazards in their deck wins. If still tied, the contestant who triggered the finale wins.

*Section 13.1: The show is not responsible for contestants lost to deep space. Or found in deep space, for that matter.*

## 14. Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Fame** | Victory points. First to 25 triggers the finale. Most Fame wins. |
| **Credits** | Currency for buying cards and installing them. Reset each turn. |
| **Power** | Energy in your ship's systems. Spent for abilities and missions. Resets each turn. |
| **Deck** | Your personal draw pile. Grows as you buy cards. |
| **Discard** | Where played and discarded cards go. Reshuffled into deck when deck is empty. |
| **Trash** | Permanently remove a card from the game. Gone forever. |
| **Install** | Place a card on a system slot for a persistent per-turn bonus. |
| **Hazard** | Bad cards opponents put in your deck. Clear them or suffer. |
| **Station** | Locations 1, 3, 5 — where markets are. |
| **Mission** | Challenges at each location. Spend power to complete for Fame. |
| **Trophy** | Mission reward with a passive trigger ability. |
| **Gear** | Mission reward with a conditional per-turn bonus. |
| **Bolt** | Mission reward with an immediate one-time bonus. |
